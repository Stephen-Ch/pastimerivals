Pastime Rivals

Pick any pitcher and any batter from baseball history and see who wins, with real, era-adjusted odds.

How to play

  • Pick a pitcher and a batter from any era.
  • Tap Pitch to play the at-bat. Every result comes from the two players' real season stats.
  • Tap Simulate 1,000 to see what usually happens in that matchup.
  • Play an inning against the computer, or challenge a friend with a link.
