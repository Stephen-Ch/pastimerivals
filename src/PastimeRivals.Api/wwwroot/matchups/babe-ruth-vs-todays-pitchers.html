How would Babe Ruth do against today's pitchers?

It is one of baseball's favorite arguments. Today's aces throw harder than anyone Ruth ever saw. Ruth hit more home runs than whole teams. In 2025, Tarik Skubal and Paul Skenes were both asked how they would pitch to him.

We ran the numbers. Short answer: Ruth strikes out a lot, but he still hits home runs.

1,000 trips to the plate

  • Against Skubal: 481 strikeouts, 69 home runs, 105 singles, 19 doubles, 4 triples, 58 walks, 3 hit by pitch, 261 outs in play.
  • Against Skenes: 414 strikeouts, 63 home runs, 101 singles, 36 doubles, 2 triples, 109 walks, 1 hit by pitch, 274 outs in play.
  • Against Pedro: 588 strikeouts, 64 home runs, 74 singles, 33 doubles, 2 triples, 73 walks, 166 outs in play.

Ruth in 1927

192 hits, 60 home runs, 137 walks and 90 strikeouts in 691 trips to the plate. He hit home runs at more than 9 times his league's rate. He also struck out at about twice his league's rate. People forget that part: Ruth swung hard and missed a lot.

The three pitchers

  • Tarik Skubal, 2025: 241 strikeouts, 33 walks and 18 home runs allowed in 748 batters faced. He throws left-handed, like Ruth hit.
  • Paul Skenes, 2025: 216 strikeouts, 42 walks and 11 home runs allowed in 733 batters faced. He throws right-handed.
  • Pedro Martinez, 1999: 313 strikeouts, 37 walks and 9 home runs allowed in 835 batters faced. He struck out batters at more than twice his league's rate.

Why the strikeouts pile up

We compare each player to his own league, then meet them on neutral ground. Ruth struck out about twice as often as his league. Pedro struck out batters more than twice as often as his. Two big edges in the same direction multiply, so the result is extreme.

Skubal adds one more thing: he is a lefty facing a lefty. Batters usually do worse against pitchers who throw from the same side. That pushes Ruth's strikeouts higher against Skubal than against Skenes, even though Skenes is a strikeout pitcher too.

Why the home runs survive

Ruth's home run edge is the biggest number on his card. No pitcher here takes it away. Even against Pedro, who almost never gave up home runs, Ruth homers about once every 19 trips.

How to read these odds

These are chances, not predictions. A 46.7% strikeout chance means Ruth strikes out a bit less than half the time. Over a few at-bats, anything can happen. That is the fun of playing it.

Questions

Would Babe Ruth hit modern pitching?

By our numbers, yes, but with far more strikeouts than in 1927. He still homers 5 to 8 times in 100 trips to the plate.

Who is the toughest for Ruth?

Pedro Martinez in 1999. Ruth reaches base about 1 time in 4.

Is 58% strikeouts realistic?

Maybe not. Our tests show the model works on average. Pairings this extreme never happened, so no one can test them. Play it and judge for yourself.
